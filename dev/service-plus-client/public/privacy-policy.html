Privacy Policy

For Service+ · Last updated 11 September 2026

Service+ is a service-centre management application operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("we", "us"). It is used by electronics repair and service businesses ("service centres") to manage repair jobs, inventory, invoicing and customer communication. This policy explains what personal information the application handles, why, and what choices people have.

Who controls the data

Each service centre that uses Service+ decides what customer information to enter and how it is used; that business is the data controller for its own records. We operate and host the application on its behalf, and process that information only to provide the service.

Information the application handles

  • Customer contact details — name, mobile number, email address and postal address, as entered by service-centre staff.
  • Device and service records — brand, product, model, serial number, purchase and warranty dates, reported faults, work performed, parts used, and related notes.
  • Billing records — invoices, payments and receipts relating to a repair.
  • Staff account information — the name, username, email address and role of people who log in to the application.
  • Message delivery records — when a notification was sent to a customer, and whether it was delivered, read or failed.

Customer information is entered by service-centre staff from their own records. The application does not buy, scrape or otherwise acquire contact lists.

How the information is used

  • To record and manage repair jobs, parts, invoices and payments.
  • To send customers notifications about their own repair — job received, repair completed, ready for collection, payment receipts and invoices.
  • To send warranty-expiry reminders where a service centre holds warranty information for a customer's device, and to record a customer's response if they ask to be contacted.
  • To produce the service centre's own operational and financial reports.

WhatsApp messaging

Service+ sends customer notifications over the WhatsApp Business Platform, operated by Meta. To deliver a message, the recipient's mobile number and the specific details shown in that message (for example a job number, an amount due, or a device and warranty expiry date) are transmitted to Meta. Meta's handling of that information is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

Messages are sent using message templates reviewed in advance. Every link in a message is single-purpose and time-limited. Reminder messages include an unsubscribe link: using it stops that type of message to that number permanently, and the choice is recorded against the customer's record.

Sharing

Information is not sold, rented or shared for advertising. It is disclosed only to: the service centre whose records they are; service providers that operate parts of the application on our behalf (hosting, email delivery, and WhatsApp message delivery as described above); and where we are required to do so by law.

Retention

Records are retained for as long as the service centre maintains its account, as its business and statutory records require. A service centre may correct or delete records it has entered. On request, and subject to any legal retention obligation, we will delete or return a service centre's data when its account is closed.

Security

Access requires an account and password, and each staff member sees only what their assigned role permits. Each service centre's records are held in a separate database. Traffic between the browser and our servers is encrypted in transit. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the information held.

Your choices

  • Stop receiving reminders — use the unsubscribe link in any warranty reminder, or ask the service centre to remove you.
  • Access, correction or deletion — contact the service centre that holds your record, or write to us at the address below and we will pass the request on.

Children

The application is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated to service centres using the application.
